# What head angle do `yaw` and `roll` really mean? (lookaway, side-eye, huh under head turn)

## Question
`faceFeatures` takes `yaw` from the nose-tip position between the cheek edges (a 2D ratio) and `roll` from the eye-corner line
in the image. `face-roll-aspect` covered frame shape. Not covered: a head that is turned, and a phone held above or below the
face (the normal selfie). Which true angles do the thresholds correspond to, and does a turned head fake a tilt?

## Method
`lab/head-yaw/sim.mjs` (output in `output.txt`): rigid 5-point head (nose 1, eye corners 33/263, cheeks 234/454), rotated by
yaw, pitch, roll, orthographic projection, then the real `faceFeatures()` and real MEMES scores. Algebra gives
yaw_feature = (p/w)·tan(true yaw), p = nose protrusion in front of the cheek line, w = cheek half-width. I did not look up
the canonical-mesh depths, so p is swept (5/7/9 cm, w = 7.5). Pitch is rotation of the head about its horizontal axis,
equivalent to the phone being above or below.

## Results
- True yaw for a feature yaw of 0.35 / 0.6 (lookaway starts / full): p=5 → 31° / 42°; p=7 → 21° / 33°; p=9 → 17° / 28°.
  Lookaway scores 0.5 at 22–36° true yaw depending only on the unmeasured nose depth. Side-eye (gaze) is half off at 18–31°
  and dead at 23–37°. The feature is not linear: it is tan, so 40° gives 0.56–1.0 and 60° gives 1.1–2.1 (no problem, clamped).
- Fake roll: apparent image roll ≈ atan(tan(yaw)·sin(pitch)). At yaw 30° and pitch ±20° it reads ±11°, at yaw 40° ±16°,
  at yaw 50° ±22°. Pitch of 10° (barely noticeable) at 40° yaw gives 8°. With a true tilt the error adds: true 0/10/20/30° at
  yaw 30°, pitch 20° measures 11/21/31/41°.
- Consequence for `huh` with raised brows and no tilt: score 0.33–0.34 at yaw 30°/pitch 30° and yaw 40°/pitch 20°; 0.85–1.0 at
  yaw 50° with pitch 20–30°. Normally `lookaway` (full at ≥33°) wins that, but it carries `not(anyHand)`: with a hand in frame
  (e.g. waving while looking away) huh is free to win. `browsRaised`, used by the shocked/fear memes, also drops out
  (its |roll| 8–16° off ramp), so a turned head with raised brows loses shocked.

## What the planner should do next
1. Do not trust the lookaway/side-eye angle until the nose depth is measured: use a real face-landmark dump (the face-dump
   tool) at known turn angles, or the 3D canonical mesh depths (not looked up here). Our range is a 14° spread.
2. If `huh` matters, suppress it when |yaw| is large (e.g. `not(ramp(|yaw|, 0.3, 0.5))`) or correct roll by using the 3D eye
   vector (landmark z) instead of the 2D one. A one-line term is enough for the simulated false huh.
3. Field check (owner/phone, M2): selfie with 30–40° head turn, phone slightly below, raised brows: does it show huh?
