# yippee (both hands up): only open palms can win it, and it needs headroom

## Question
`handsUp` = two hands with wrist (0) and middle knuckle (9) above the forehead-top landmark (10); `yippee = handsUp`. Earlier notes put one hand above the head (hand-near-face) but never the two-hand cheer. Can a user actually trigger yippee, and which hand shapes win it?

## Method
`lab/hands-up/sim.mjs`, `scale.mjs`. Real HaGRID v2 test hands (single-hand images, 300 per class: palm, fist, like), rigidly placed with wrists at chosen heights beside a synthetic face (top y=.30, chin .62, 9:16), second hand an x-mirror. Real `MEMES` scores, steady-state argmax with the 0.35 floor, neutral face. `scale.mjs` scales hands about the wrist, since HaGRID hands are smaller than selfie hands (k=1 HaGRID size, k=1.5–2 assumed selfie size; unmeasured).

## Results
1. **Threshold is sharp and shape-blind.** Two palms with wrists 0.02 frame heights above the forehead landmark: yippee 100%; at or below it: no yippee (wizard/stare). No gradient: `handsUp` is 0/1.
2. **Only open (or unrecognised) hands can win yippee.** Cheering with two fists wrists at y=.20: handsUp = 100% but shown meme is fistbump 96% (thumbs 3%, point 1%), yippee 0%. Two thumbs-ups: thumbs 99%. Palm+fist: fistbump 96%. Cause: yippee scores 1 like the gesture meme, and `choose` keeps the first max in list order (yippee is last), the same tie mechanism as in `reachability`/`hand-near-face`. Only two open palms (100%) win it (and palms that miss the open-palm test also still reach yippee, since handsUp ignores shape).
3. **Headroom.** Wrist-to-highest-landmark of an upright hand, median 0.15 frame heights (HaGRID size; p10 .10, p90 .23). For the whole hand to stay in frame with wrist above the forehead (top y .30): 99% at k=1, 78% at k=1.5, 48% at k=2, 27% at k=2.5 (median headroom needed 0.15/0.23/0.31/0.38). With a face top at y=.20 only 78/~40/~15% fit. How MediaPipe handles hands cut off by the frame edge is unmeasured here (it often still returns landmarks, clamped or extrapolated).

## Reading
Yippee is reachable for open palms with a normal selfie framing, but a "hands up, fists clenched, celebrating" pose (the more natural cheer) gives fistbump. The README says "both hands up above your head" without a shape.

## What the planner should do next
- Make yippee beat single-hand memes when handsUp: add `not(f.handsUp)` to fist/thumbs/shaka/point/think scoring (or have `handFeatures` zero the per-hand flags when handsUp). Acceptance: two fists/thumbs-ups above the forehead show yippee in a unit test; one fist unchanged.
- Consider a soft ramp for handsUp (distance above forehead / face height) rather than a 0/1 step, and document the required headroom in the hint ("keep your hands in frame").
- Phone smoke clip with two raised hands to check how MediaPipe behaves at the top frame edge (needs M2).
